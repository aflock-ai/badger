    Spend and output: enforced by the broker on every call

    For people and workloads. Each person and workload has their own allowance; their own limit wins over the default. Days reset at 00:00 UTC; months at 00:00 UTC on the 1st. From 80% of a monthly or daily limit, the broker adds a warning to each answer (header X-Broker-Quota-Warning; workspaces do not show it, so watch Needs attention on Overview). At 100%, new requests are refused (HTTP 429): a pipeline is told which limit it reached and when it resets; a workspace user is told to ask an administrator. Every change is kept in the audit log with who made it and why.

    Agent behavior: applied by the managed agent when a session starts

    For people only. Workloads call the models directly, with no managed agent, so these do not apply to them. The agent reads its values when a session starts. A change applies to new sessions only; a running session keeps the values it started with. These guard against runaway sessions; spend is limited above.
