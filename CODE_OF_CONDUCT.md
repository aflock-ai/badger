# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the work, and do not harass anyone.

## Scope

The code of conduct applies in this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions and review
comments, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to security@testifysec.com until the project lists a separate conduct
contact. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers who are the subject of a report will not
take part in handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who violate
this code of conduct. Repeated or serious violations lead to a permanent ban.
